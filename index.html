Ne düzelttim

Bu resmi Pointercrate değil. Sıra, 23 Ekim 2026 sabahı Pointercrate API'sindeki Rated+ yerleştirmesinden geliyor. Üstüne kendi kuralımı koydum. Zorluğu kafama göre yeniden yazmadım; veri olmayan bir kaydırma, düzelttiğim hatadan daha kötü olur.

1. Unrated artık yok sayılmıyor

Klasik liste yalnız rate'li level alır. Bugün Old Orochi, Silent clubstep'ten zor diye #17'ye kondu ama klasik sitede hâlâ yok. Kızıl zorluk sırasında onu gösterir. Puan yazmaz: rate gelmeden skor tahtasına puan basmak, Rated+'ın henüz oturmamış tarafı.

2. Aynı isim, iki level

İki ayrı “Thinking Space II” var. CairoX'inki klasik #4. CoCyMain'inki bugün unrated #39. Diğer listeler ikisini de aynı adla basıyor. Kızıl yayınlayanı ismin yanına koyar.

3. Puan formülü

Birçok 2026 rehberi puanı (ilerleme/100)×(eşik/100)×500 diye anlatıyor. GRIEF'te %76 rekor bu formülle 288.80 puan çıkar. Pointercrate'in sayfada yazdığı skor 35.00. Doğrusu: tamamlama puanı yalnız sıraya bağlı; eşikteki rekor o puanın %10'u; %100'de tamamı; ara doğrusal. Extended'da yüzde rekoru yok, yalnız bitiş.

4. Eşik, zorluk değildir

Ultra Paracosm eşiği %37. Bu level'ın yumuşak olduğu anlamına gelmez; zor kısım erken biter. Through The Gates %90, Penumbral %80, Master of Puppets %83, Maniacal Chains %85: zor kısım sonda, yüksek yüzde bile rekor sayılmaz. Ana listedeki bu beşine “eşik” etiketi koydum. Extended'da %100 eşiği kuraldır, aykırılık değil.

5. Bugün kim düştü, gizli değil

Rated+, 10 unrated'i top 150'ye sokunca klasik #142–150 arasını kendi legacy'sine itti. Starlight Summit klasik listede hâlâ #150 ve 18.71 puan. Rated+'ta #159, puan yok. Kızıl buna “sınırda” der. Tek sayı gösteren liste bu çelişkiyi siler.

6. Keyifsiz diye sıra uydurmadım

Silent clubstep, Acheron ve Avernus yıllardır keyif ve “liste için üretildi” tartışmasının içindeler. Acheron'un 2022'de #2'ye konması listenin en çok dislike alan hareketi. Bugünkü yerlerini oyuncu karşılaştırması belirliyor; ben onu bozmadım, notu ekledim.

What I fixed

This is not the official Pointercrate. The order comes from the Rated+ placement in the Pointercrate API on the morning of 23 October 2026. On top of it I put my own rules. I did not rewrite difficulty to taste; a shift with no data behind it would be worse than the mistake I am fixing.

1. Unrated is no longer ignored

The classic list only takes rated levels. Today Old Orochi was placed at #17 as harder than Silent clubstep, but the classic site still does not have it. Crimson shows it in the difficulty order. It awards no points: putting points on the scoreboard before a rate arrives is the part of Rated+ that has not settled yet.

2. Same name, two levels

There are two different “Thinking Space II”s. CairoX’s is classic #4. CoCyMain’s is unrated #39 today. Other lists print both under the same name. Crimson puts the publisher next to the name.

3. The points formula

Many 2026 guides describe points as (progress/100)×(threshold/100)×500. On GRIEF, a 76% record gives 288.80 points with that formula. The score Pointercrate prints on the page is 35.00. The truth: completion points depend only on the rank; a record at the threshold is 10% of that; at 100% it is all of it; linear in between. Extended has no percentage records, only completions.

4. Threshold is not difficulty

Ultra Paracosm’s threshold is 37%. That does not mean the level is soft; the hard part ends early. Through The Gates 90%, Penumbral 80%, Master of Puppets 83%, Maniacal Chains 85%: the hard part is at the end, and even a high percentage does not count as a record. I put a “Threshold” label on these five in the main list. In Extended a 100% threshold is the rule, not an outlier.

5. Who fell off today is not hidden

When Rated+ pushed 10 unrated levels into the top 150, it moved classic #142–150 into its own legacy. Starlight Summit is still classic #150 with 18.71 points. In Rated+ it is #159, with no points. Crimson calls this “borderline”. A list that shows a single number hides this contradiction.

6. I did not invent a rank because a level isn’t fun

Silent clubstep, Acheron and Avernus have been in the “fun” and “made for the list” debate for years. Acheron’s 2022 placement at #2 is the list’s most-disliked move. Where they sit today is set by player comparison; I did not break that, I added a note.

Ranking rules

  1. Crimson order = Rated+ order (last checked: 24 October 2026).
  2. Points = if the level is rated and its classic rank is 1–150, that rank’s official curve. Otherwise 0.
  3. The Main / Extended / Legacy tiers follow the Crimson order (1–75, 76–150, 151–200).
  4. Publisher and verifier are written separately. The name on the list is often not the only creator.
  5. Instead of Legacy’s “unordered dump”, the 50 most recently dropped levels are shown. Older ones are not in this file.

Sources

The order comes from Pointercrate’s Rated+ list. Pointercrate is sometimes slow to update, so the Global Demonlist (demonlist.org) is used as a second opinion: running the comparison in the “Stats” tab shows levels in the Global top 150 that are missing from this file, and big rank gaps between Crimson and Global. Each level’s Global rank is also added to the list. The two lists are ranked by different methods, so a gap is not automatically an error.

Submitting records

There is no “I beat it” self-declaration. To submit a record, pick the level and enter your player name, percentage and a proof video. The form opens a pre-filled GitHub issue; when it arrives the list maintainer is notified by GitHub, watches the video, and an approved record is added.
